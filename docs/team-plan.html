Team Build Plan

Four people, two pairs. The Surface pair owns infrastructure, MCP, UI and outside services; the Engine pair owns the negotiation brain. Each starts on its riskiest piece and builds against a mock of the other, then the pairs bridge. Switch plans to compare idle time and collisions. Click any block or bridge for its owner, files, mocks, and the test that says it's done. Hours are relative (H0 = start).
